How well do statistical charts reveal differences between groups?

In this study you will be shown pairs of statistical charts and asked to judge whether the two charts appear to come from different underlying sources.

There is a short training section before the main study. The whole study takes around 10–15 minutes, and there are no right or wrong answers.
